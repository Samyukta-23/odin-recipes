Masala Buttermilk

Description

When your internal mainframe is overheating from spicy data inputs, deploy this liquid cooling system immediately. Packed with spices and herbs, it acts as a refreshing antivirus scan for your gut, purging bloating and system lag with a single gulp. It is the ultimate open-source digestive patch, running smoothly in the background while you process questionable lifestyle choices. Keep this beverage running on a continuous loop to avoid a total gastric meltdown.

Ingredients

  • Base OS: 2 cups plain yogurt (curd)
  • Liquid Medium: 2 cups chilled water
  • Antivirus Agent: 1 green chili, deseeded and finely chopped
  • System Optimizer: ½ inch ginger, crushed or grated
  • Background Process: 2 tablespoons fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped
  • Network Patch: 1 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • Firewall Salt: ½ teaspoon salt

Installation Guide

  1. Initialize Base: Pour the yogurt into a deep mixing vessel and whisk vigorously until all lumps are cleared from the directory.
  2. Inject Spices: Toss in the finely chopped green chili, crushed ginger, coriander leaves, roasted cumin powder, and salts.
  3. Dilute the Mainframe: Pour in the chilled water and use a traditional churner or a high-speed blender to run a 30-second emulsification script.
  4. Serve System Refresh: Pour the liquid cooling system into glasses, garnish with a pinch of cumin powder, and deploy chilled immediately.
