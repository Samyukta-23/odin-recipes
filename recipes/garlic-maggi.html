Ingredients

  • Hardware Architecture: 250g ramen, udon, or spaghetti noodles
  • Processing Fluid: 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • Overclocking Agent: 10-12 cloves of garlic, finely minced
  • Data Stream Modifier: 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • Open-Source Glaze: 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Bandwidth Enhancer: 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • System UI Finish: 2 tablespoons green onions, chopped
  • Visual Filter: 1 tablespoon parmesan cheese, grated

Installation Guide

  1. Compile Noodles: Drop your noodles into boiling water, running the standard packet optimization instructions until they are al dente, then drain and save a splash of noodle water.
  2. Boot Up the Garlic: Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat and dump in the minced garlic, frying it until your kitchen olfactory sensors register maximum bandwidth.
  3. Inject Sauce Payload: Stir in the oyster sauce, soy sauce, and brown sugar, whisking until the liquid medium is smoothly integrated.
  4. Merge Files: Toss the drained noodles directly into the garlic matrix, adding a splash of the reserved noodle water to prevent system drying.
  5. Final UI Render: Turn off the heat, throw in the green onions and parmesan cheese, and toss until the application is fully coated.
