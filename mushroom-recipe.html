Mushroom starter

Description

A cloud-native appetizer marinated in a rich middleware of curd, ginger-garlic paste, and heavy spices, then successfully deployed with a thick layer of fried onions. It is a highly volatile, bloatware-level flavor bomb that guarantees your digestive mainframe will experience significant lag but deep satisfaction. Every single bite delivers a heavy payload of heat that tests your tolerance limits. Treat it like a beta software release: incredibly exciting to interact with, but you might pay for it in the next deployment cycle.

Ingredients

  • Beta Test Subject: 250g button mushrooms, cleaned and halved
  • Middleware Matrix: ½ cup thick curd (yogurt)
  • System Compiler: 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Spicy Payload: 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder
  • Thermal Accelerator: ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • Background Logic: ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Acidity Check: 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Optimization Binding: 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • UI Overlay: ½ cup fried onions (birista)
  • Garnish Script: Fresh coriander leaves

Installation Guide

  1. Sanitize Assets: Wipe your mushrooms clean with a damp towel to remove soil bugs, then cut them into clean halves.
  2. Inject Middleware: In a large bowl, whisk the curd, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, garam masala, turmeric, lemon juice, and cornstarch into a thick marinade.
  3. Run Marination Script: Toss the mushrooms into the spicy curd matrix, ensuring 100% coverage, and let the background process sit for 20 minutes.
  4. Thermal Execution: Heat oil in a pan, drop the marinated mushrooms in a single layer, and stir-fry over high heat until the water evaporates and the coating sticks to the hardware.
  5. Deploy Fried Onions: Toss in the fried onions during the last minute of cooking so they merge with the spicy mushroom texture.
  6. Final UI Finish: Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot before the crisp textures expire.
